<p><strong>ABSTRACT</strong></p>



<p>Providing excellent service in non-life insurance is crucial. It makes customers happy,<br>keeps them returning, and sets companies apart. This study determined the service<br>quality of non-life insurance companies in Butuan City, Philippines. A pure descriptive<br>research design was used for this study. The researchers surveyed insured (30) and<br>prospect clients (30) to gather diverse perspectives aligned to the five dimensions of<br>service quality: reliability, responsiveness, assurance, empathy, and tangibles.<br>Frequency and Percentage were used to determine the Percentage from the profile of<br>the insured and prospective clients; the Mean was also used to determine the average<br>measured in the study variables. The study showed that assurance was rated the<br>highest among the five service quality dimensions. At the same time, reliability and<br>responsiveness offered straightforward ways for the insurance companies to improve<br>for insured and prospective clients. Non-life insurance companies can strengthen<br>customer loyalty and satisfaction by speeding up claims processing and being more<br>responsive.</p>
