<p>The PECIT marketing team is accelerating its efforts to drive the growth of our institution and attract more enrollees. Our dynamic team, led by official members and spokespersons Carren C. Gallardo, Keith Mabaga Johnson, and John Michael Bravo Egot, along with the dedicated contributions of Sir Carlo Jay and Knack Almeda, is working tirelessly to expand our reach.</p>



<p>Our mission spans across vibrant locales such as Pagatpatan, Pequeno, Doongan, and Bonbon, Libertad, Tungao, Lumbocan, Kinamlutan, Taligaman, as well as integrated and national high schools, where we engage with Grade 10 and Grade 12 Senior High Learners. Through meticulously crafted marketing campaigns and personalized interactions, we aim to showcase the exceptional educational opportunities and vibrant communities that define PECIT.</p>



<p>Our goal is to showcase the exceptional educational opportunities and vibrant community at PECIT, ensuring that prospective students and their families understand the value and benefits of joining our institution. Through strategic outreach and personalized engagement, our marketing team is fostering strong connections within these communities, highlighting the unique offerings and supportive environment at PECIT.</p>



<p>Stay tuned as the PECIT marketing team continues to innovate and execute strategies that will not only increase enrollment but also strengthen our institution’s reputation. With their relentless drive and passion, they are paving the way for a brighter future for all PECIT students. Join us on this exciting journey and witness the positive impact of their efforts in every corner of our community!</p>
