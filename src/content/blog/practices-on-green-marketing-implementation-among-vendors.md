<p><strong>ABSTRACT</strong></p>



<p>This study examined the practices of vendors on green marketing implementation in<br>Langihan Public Market Butuan City, Philippines. The researchers employed a<br>descriptive-correlation research design among 50 vendors of Langihan Public Market,<br>and purposive sampling was used in the study. The study used a survey questionnaire<br>with two distinct parts. The first part provided information about the respondents&#8217;<br>profiles, while the second part, the survey proper, posed questions to investigate the<br>topic of interest. Frequency/percentage, Mean, and Pearson-r were the statistical tools<br>used in treating the problem. The study found a significant correlation between the<br>practices and the extent of green marketing implementation. In addition, the findings<br>highlighted that the vendors need more knowledge and skills about green marketing<br>and its implementation. However, they have the attitude to implementing green<br>marketing by providing proper knowledge and teaching some skills. The study<br>suggests that the LGU provides training for vendors and strict implementation, the<br>BLGU assesses awareness and explores strategies, vendors enhance their skills, and<br>academic institutions support green marketing.</p>
