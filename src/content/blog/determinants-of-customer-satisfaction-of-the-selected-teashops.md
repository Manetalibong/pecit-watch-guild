<p><strong>ABSTRACT</strong>:<br>This study aimed to determine the relationship between the determinants and<br>customer satisfaction of the selected tea shops. The study used a descriptive correlation research design and was conducted in three selected tea shops: JB Bubbly<br>Tea House, SweeTea House of MilkTea, and Burp&#8217;s Koi SnackHouse. The study&#8217;s<br>respondents were the (90) customers who were the selected Tea Shops&#8217; guests. The<br>researchers used a questionnaire to determine the determinants and customer<br>satisfaction level. Mean and Pearson-r were used to treat the problem. The study&#8217;s<br>findings showed that the relationships between various determinants and customer<br>satisfaction reveal food quality, reliability, and price. These factors exhibit a significant<br>relationship with customer satisfaction, highlighting their importance in driving positive<br>customer experiences. Accessibility shows a substantial relationship with customer<br>satisfaction in terms of food quality. This implies that ease of access influences<br>customers&#8217; perception of the food experience. Social media marketing strategies<br>showcase a significant relationship with all other measured determinants (food quality,<br>service quality, reliability, and price) except for ambiance. This implies that effective<br>social media strategies can boost customer satisfaction across various aspects of the<br>tea shop experience.</p>
