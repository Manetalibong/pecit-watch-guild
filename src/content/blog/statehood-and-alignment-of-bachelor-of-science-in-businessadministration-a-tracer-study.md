<p><strong>Keywords:</strong> Statehood, Alignment, BSBA, Descriptive Follow-up, Philippines</p>



<p><strong>ABSTRACT</strong><br>This study investigated the employment status and job alignment of Bachelor of Science in<br>Business Administration (BSBA) graduates from the Philippine Electronics and<br>Communication Institute of Technology (PECIT) between 2016 and 2021. This study<br>used a descriptive follow-up research design. Purposive sampling was used to identify<br>respondents who participated in the study. A survey questionnaire was used in the<br>study, sent through the respondents&#8217; social media accounts. Frequency and<br>percentage were applied to analyze the data. Most respondents were aged 25-30,<br>predominantly female cohort, mostly graduated in 2019, and are single. Data reveals<br>that most respondents had prior employment in the private sector, though career<br>details were not always disclosed. Income primarily fell within the 11,001-20,000<br>bracket, and job alignment perceptions varied among respondents. The study provides<br>valuable insights into the profile and professional background of PECIT&#8217;s BSBA<br>graduates</p>
