<p><strong>ABSTRACT</strong><br>Pets have been part of our lives since ancient times. In recent days, they have become<br>an integral part of our families.  In fact, we tend not to differentiate between pets and<br>human family members. This study used qualitative method and focuses on acquiring a pet dog: Affecting the decision making of a prospective dog owner as perceived by selected dog lovers. The<br>Study has selected twenty respondents dog lover in Butuan City. Majority of the<br>respondents are female. These are the indicators; physical appearance has the<br>highest weighted mean at 3.4(Very High Preference); followed by assurance at 3.3<br>(Very High Preference) and the last is Behavior at 3.2 (High Preference). Test of<br>significant difference in preference using gender as the differentiating variable.<br>Therefore, acquiring a Pet Dog decision making of Prospective Dog Owners perceived<br>that they highly preferred to know the pet dog in terms of physical appearance,<br>behavior and assurance. Gender is not a differentiating variable in terms of preference.<br>This study recommended that the basis in acquiring dogs and any pet are based on<br>the perception mentioned, and PECIT administrator can use the results of this study<br>as basis in making a school program activities to the marketing management students<br>related to effective marketing strategy in engaging pet business.</p>
