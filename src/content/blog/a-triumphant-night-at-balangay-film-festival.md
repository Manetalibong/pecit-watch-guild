<p>Butuan City, Philippines – It was a night to remember for the Philippine Electronics and Communications Institute of Technology (PECIT) as its official film entry,</p>



<p>“Si Along at ang Ben10 na Bag,” made waves at the Balangay Independent Film and Theater Festival on November 29, 2024—bringing home not one, but two prestigious awards!</p>



<p>The film won the Audience Choice Award, a true reflection of the overwhelming support from viewers who connected with its heartfelt story. Adding to the celebration, Arju Naranjo delivered a powerful and emotional performance as Along, earning him the title of Best Actor.<br>A special mention goes to Sir Mark Hingco, whose incredible storytelling earned him a Best Story nomination, proving that PECIT’s creative talent is one to watch.</p>



<p>The team’s journey was made even more special during the free screening at Robinsons Cinema @2, where a packed theater, filled with excited faces, reminded everyone of the magic of independent storytelling. The cheers, laughter, and tears from the audience made every effort worthwhile.</p>



<p>This victory belongs not just to the cast and crew, but to everyone who supported, voted, and believed in PECIT’s vision. To God be the glory!
