<p>In a heartwarming display of community and collaboration, the Philippine Electronics and Communication Institute of Technology (PECIT) brought together students from the College of Teachers Education, College of Business Administration, College of Hospitality Management, and College of Science and Information Systems for the vibrant \&#8221;Able dos Mil Bente Tres\&#8221; event this August. Under the guidance of Academic Head Director Romeo Approvechar, CESO V, and dedicated officers from various departments, the event fostered a sense of belonging and shared purpose among students.The \&#8221;Able dos Mil Bente Tres\&#8221; event was more than just a series of workshops and presentations; it was a celebration of creativity and connection. Students participated in engaging activities designed to enhance their skills while also allowing them to express their unique talents. The atmosphere buzzed with excitement as participants collaborated, shared ideas, and formed friendships that transcended their individual disciplines.</p>



<p>Director Approvechar shared his thoughts on the event, saying, “This gathering is not just about academics; it’s about building a community. By uniting students from different colleges, we’re creating an environment where everyone can learn from each other and grow together. It’s about preparing them for the real world, where teamwork and diverse perspectives are essential.”</p>



<p>The event saw an enthusiastic turnout, with students diving into interactive sessions, networking opportunities, and team-building exercises. The collaboration among the colleges highlighted the importance of interdisciplinary learning, showing that when students come together, they can tackle challenges in innovative ways.</p>



<p>As PECIT continues to nurture academic excellence and community spirit, the success of the \&#8221;Able dos Mil Bente Tres\&#8221; event stands as a testament to the power of unity in education. It marks a significant step towards creating a vibrant, inclusive environment where every student feels valued and empowered to thrive.</p>
