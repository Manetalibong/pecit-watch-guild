<p><strong>ABSTRACT</strong><br>This study determined the Students’ Perception on Online Class towards Academic<br>Achievement based on their experiences. To know if online class is effective to the<br>students’ academic achievement, or if it only gave them a hard time leading them to<br>acquire low grades. The specific sub-problems included, were the profile of the<br>respondents in terms of age, sex, their perception on attending online class, and also<br>their academic achievement. This research was conducted by survey method through<br>online with use of Google Forms and distributed to the 30 second year Bachelor of<br>Elementary Education students in the first semester of School Year 2021-2022 in<br>Philippine Electronics and Communication Institute of Technology. It utilized<br>quantitative research method, in order to know the different perceptions of the students<br>in attending online class towards their academic achievement. Based on the findings<br>of the study, it was found out that most perceptions of the second year Bachelor of<br>Elementary Education students in online class was they submitted their tasks on time,<br>and performed good in the Online Class. This was because most of their responses in<br>the survey questionnaires are Agree. Based on the result, the Philippine Electronics and<br>Communication Institute of Technology (PECIT) administration should have an internet<br>hub or a room, on where the students can connect to internet for educational purposes.<br>The teachers should be innovative and help the students improve their technological<br>skills and monitor their academic performance. The students should be self-reliant and<br>at the same time be collaborative, and have self-disciplined on using technologies, no<br>matter what modality is offered, the students should focus on how to meet the demands<br>on their studies. The project on putting an internet hub has been done to cater students’<br>needs in internet connectivity.</p>
