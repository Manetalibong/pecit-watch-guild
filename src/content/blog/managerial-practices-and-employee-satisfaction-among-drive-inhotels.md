<p><strong>Keywords:</strong> Managerial Practices, Employee Satisfaction, Drive-in Hotels, Descriptive Correlation, Philippines</p>



<p><strong>ABSTRACT</strong><br>This study investigated the relationship between managerial practices and employee<br>satisfaction. This study used a descriptive-correlation research design. The study<br>utilized a survey questionnaire to capture the respondents&#8217; profiles, measure<br>organizational practices, and assess various facets of employee satisfaction.<br>Statistical tools such as frequency/percentage, mean, and Pearson-r were used to<br>analyze the data. This study reveals that the correlation between managerial practices<br>and employee satisfaction in drive-in hotels has significant relationships (P&lt;0.05). The<br>findings suggested that effective planning, organizing, staffing, leading, and controlling<br>practices contributed to positive employee satisfaction in various dimensions, but the<br>impact may have varied across specific areas. The results highlighted the importance<br>of considering different managerial practices to enhance employee satisfaction in<br>drive-in hotels.<br><br></p>
