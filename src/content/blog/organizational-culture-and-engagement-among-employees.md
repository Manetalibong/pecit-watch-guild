<p>College of Business Management Education, PECIT, Agusan Del Norte, 8600 Philippines<br><br><strong>Corresponding Author: </strong>Maria Cecilia Z. Matillano, Phd<br><strong>Date Received:</strong> January 2024 Revised: February 2024 Accepted : March 2024<br><br><strong>Keywords:</strong> Organizational Culture, Engagement, Effectiveness, Descriptive Correlation Design, Mean Score, Pearson Correlation, Regular Employees</p>



<p>This study investigated the relationship between organizational culture and employee<br>engagement at the Philippine Electronics and Communication Institute of Technology,<br>Inc. (PECIT, Inc.). The researchers used a descriptive-correlational research design<br>to describe the organizational culture and engagement and determine the relationship<br>between the study variables. The study&#8217;s respondents were regular employees at<br>PECIT, Inc., and thirty (30) respondents participated. They chose them because they<br>experienced firsthand knowledge of the company&#8217;s culture. This study employed mean<br>scores and Pearson correlation to examine employees&#8217; perceptions of organizational<br>culture and engagement levels. The study showed that PECIT, Inc. employees boast<br>a positive corporate culture and strongly agree on empowerment, collaboration,<br>growth opportunities, and aligned values. However, dedication received the lowest<br>engagement score, though still optimistic overall. This highlights the importance of<br>fostering dedication alongside other engagement aspects for maximized productivity<br>and performance.</p>
