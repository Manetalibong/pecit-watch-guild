<p>In the spirit of unity and compassion, the Philippine Electronics and Communication Institute of Technology (PECIT School, recently undertook a heartwarming community extension initiative in Barangay San Roque, Las Nieves Agusan Del Norte. This noble endeavor, led by the visionary Founder and President, Engr. Romeo L. Zerrudo, along with the dedicated Executive Vice President, Maria Cecilia Z. Matillano, Research Director Dr. Josephine U. Acha, and NSTP Director Chuchie Carganilla, has left an indelible mark on the community.</p>



<p>The PECIT team&#8217;s commitment to a community outreach and the spirit of giving back was on full display during the Gift-Giving event. The initiative aimed to uplift the lives of the residents of Barangay San Roque, fostering a sense of solidarity and shared purpose. Engr. Romeo L. Zerrudo expressed, &#8220;At PECIT, we believe in the transformative power of service. Our school is not only dedicated to technological advancements but also to making a positive impact on the communities we serve.&#8221;</p>



<p>The event, held in collaboration with local authorities and community leaders, showcased PECIT&#8217;s dedication to creating meaningful connections beyond technological innovation. The PECIT team distributed essential items, including food packages, and educational materials, to families in need. The beneficiaries, grateful for the support, shared stories of resilience and hope in the face of challenges.</p>



<p>Executive Vice President Maria Cecilia Z. Matillano emphasized the importance of community involvement, stating, &#8220;Our responsibility extends beyond the boardroom and laboratories. It encompasses the well-being of the communities where we live and work. Through initiatives like this, we aim to contribute to the development and betterment of society.&#8221;</p>



<p>Dr. Josephine U. Acha, Research Director, highlighted the research-driven approach to community service, stating, &#8220;As an organization deeply rooted in research, we understand the unique needs of communities. Our outreach programs are designed not only to address immediate concerns but also to lay the foundation for sustainable development l.&#8221;</p>



<p>Chuchie Carganilla, NSTP Director, understand the significance of collaboration with educational institutions, stating &#8220;Working with local schools and educational partners is crucial in fostering holistic development. By engaging with the youth, we hope to inspire the next generation of leaders and advocates for positive change.&#8221;</p>



<p>The PECIT team&#8217;s successful community service in Barangay San Roque serves as a treatment to the organization&#8217;s commitment to social responsibility. Engr. Romeo L. Zerrudo, Maria Cecilia Z. Matillano, Dr. Josephine U. Acha, and Chuchie Carganilla exemplify how a cohesive and dedicated team can make a meaningful impact on the lives of others. As PECIT continues to bridge the gap between technology and community, it&#8217;s endeavors stand as a beacon of inspiration for others to follow suit, creating a ripple effect of positive change throughout the region.</p>
