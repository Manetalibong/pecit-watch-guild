<p>Like a perfectly synchronized melody, Christian Joseph Laroda and Lizbeth Pajo of PECIT took center stage at the PRISAA Caraga Regional Vocal Duet Competition 2025, their voices weaving together in a symphony of triumph. Competing against the finest vocalists from across the region, they didn’t just sing—they soared, capturing the audience’s hearts and the gold medal in a performance that echoed excellence.</p>



<p>Held on February 27–28, 2025, at Caraga State University – Cabadbaran Campus, the competition was a battleground of harmonies, where every note carried the weight of ambition and dedication. With each crescendo, Laroda and Pajo painted a masterpiece of sound, their voices blending like golden threads in a musical tapestry. Their passion, discipline, and undeniable talent transformed the stage into a realm of artistry, proving that music is not just heard—it is felt.</p>



<p>As the final note lingered in the air, a thunderous applause erupted, sealing their fate as champions and national qualifiers for the PRISAA National Games. They didn’t just win a medal; they etched their names in the legacy of PECIT’s finest talents.</p>



<p>This victory is more than just a title—it is a testament to the power of dedication, the beauty of collaboration, and the magic that happens when talent meets perseverance. As they set their sights on the national stage, one thing is certain: their voices will continue to inspire, uplift, and conquer.</p>



<p>Congratulations, Christian and Lizbeth! The stage is yours—sing on, champions!<br><br><em>by: Arnel D. Capenianes</em></p>
