<p><strong>ABSTRACT</strong><br><br>This study aimed to determine the experiences of online sellers. This study used a<br>qualitative research design, employing a phenomenological approach. The study<br>involved ten online sellers in Butuan City who served as participants. The researchers<br>used an interview guide as the research instrument to gather data. Thematic analysis<br>was used in the interpretation of the data. The data revealed that online selling brings<br>benefits like higher sales, flexible schedules, and social connections, but sellers face<br>challenges such as customer issues, financial constraints, and tech difficulties. These<br>findings show the complexity of online selling and the importance of adaptability and<br>proactivity. In conclusion, Online sellers enjoy income, flexibility, and social benefits<br>but face challenges like customer issues, scams, finances, and tech problems that<br>impact business and emotions. To thrive, resilience and adaptability are critical, and<br>successful sellers use strategies like visual appeal, tagging, scheduled posts, and<br>customer satisfaction incentives to stand out in a competitive marketplace.</p>
