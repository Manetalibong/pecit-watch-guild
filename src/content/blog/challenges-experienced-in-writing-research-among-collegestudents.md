<p><strong>ABSTRACT</strong><br>This study aimed to determine the challenges experienced in writing research among<br>college students. This study used a pure descriptive research design. This study was<br>conducted at the Philippine Electronics and Communication Institute of Technology<br>(PECIT). The study&#8217;s respondents were college students with a Bachelor of Science<br>in Business Administration, Bachelor of Elementary Education, and Bachelor of<br>Science in Hospitality Management. There were 150 students participated in the study.<br>The study used a survey questionnaire to gather data. The researchers used statistical<br>tools to treat the problems. Mean, rank, and linear regression were used in the study.<br>This study found that editing research papers emerges as the most significant hurdle<br>for college students, significantly exceeding the perceived difficulty of other research<br>steps. While selecting topics, finding resources, and other aspects are moderately<br>challenging; Editing presents a unique need for targeted support due to its perceived<br>difficulty gap. The researchers proposed a seminar-workshop to choose a research<br>topic and to enhance editing skills. The provided seems well- structured and addresses<br>the needs of various stakeholders at PECIT. This is a strong foundation for a<br>comprehensive research skills improvement plan at the Philippine Electronics and<br>Communication Institute of Technology (PECIT).</p>
