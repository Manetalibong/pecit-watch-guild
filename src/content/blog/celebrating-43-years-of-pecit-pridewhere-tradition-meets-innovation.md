<p>The PECIT community proudly commenced its 43rd Foundation Day with a vibrant celebration<br>that blended tradition and innovation. Carrying the theme “43 Years of Transformation: A<br>Journey of Metamorphosis Toward Greater Heights,” the event embodied the institution’s<br>enduring commitment to Filipino identity, academic excellence, and cultural pride.</p>



<p>The opening day transformed the campus into a lively pasyalan of Filipino heritage. Food stalls<br>offered authentic local delicacies while karaoke sessions filled the air with timeless OPM<br>classics, fostering camaraderie and a shared sense of pride. Students showcased their creativity<br>and intellect through a series of academic and artistic competitions, including skirting, fruit<br>carving, and a Quiz Bowl highlighting Filipino history and global achievements.Traditional skills<br>such as fruit carving stood proudly beside modern expressions like Esports, illustrating PECIT’s<br>mission to honor cultural heritage while embracing technological progress. Modern competitions<br>such as Esports (MLBB Tournament) and Chef Wars added a contemporary flair, symbolizing<br>PECIT’s harmonious balance between technology and creativity.</p>



<p><br>The second day radiated with passion, teamwork, and artistic expression. A colorful Parade of<br>Unity, led by faculty and students in discipline-themed attire, echoed the spirit of Filipino fiestas<br>as it concluded at the Capitol Covered Court. A Thanksgiving Mass followed, paying tribute to<br>the institution’s founders and celebrating values of bayanihan (community unity) and<br>pagmamalasakit (compassion).</p>



<p><br>During the keynote addresses, VP Ma. Cecilia Z. Matillano emphasized the importance of<br>harmonizing innovation with tradition, urging the PECIT community to “shape futures without<br>forgetting our roots.” Founder Engr. Romeo Lopez Jaena Zerudo delivered an emotional<br>retrospective, reflecting on PECIT’s transformation into a beacon of education and progress.<br>The highlight of the day was the Dance Competition: Stories Through Movement, where each<br>team creatively depicted meaningful social themes. </p>



<p>Team Panther portrayed heritage and<br>environmental stewardship, Team Eagles celebrated maternal love and family bonds, while<br>Teams Coyote and Iguana confronted corruption and resilience. Team Tamaraw championed the<br>upliftment of marginalized communities, and Team Scorpion paid homage to the beauty and<br>power of nature. These performances reflected not only talent but also PECIT’s dedication to<br>social awareness and creative storytelling.</p>



<p><br>Afternoon events celebrated literary and artistic brilliance through Oratorical Contests,<br>Storytelling, and Singing Competitions that showcased students’ eloquence, artistry, and passion.<br>As night fell, the excitement reached new heights with the much-anticipated Ms. PECIT 2025<br>pageant held at the Capitol. Each team proudly presented their stunning candidates who<br>competed with grace, intelligence, and confidence in a dazzling display of beauty, brain, and<br>elegance. The event embodied PECIT’s vision of empowering individuals who shine not only in<br>appearance but also in intellect and character.</p>



<p>As the festivities concluded, the campus buzzed with anticipation for the grand finale of Day 3.<br>The 43rd Foundation Day not only commemorated decades of growth and transformation but<br>also ignited a collective vision for the future—one where cultural pride and academic excellence<br>continue to propel PECIT toward greater heights.</p>
