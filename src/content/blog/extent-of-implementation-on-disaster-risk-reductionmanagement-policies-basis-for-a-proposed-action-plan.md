<p><strong>ABSTRACT</strong><br>This study determined the extent of implementation of the disaster risk reduction<br>management (DRRM) policies for typhoons and floods in Barangay Buhangin,<br>Butuan City. As a community prone to these hazards, it is essential for the barangay<br>to have effective policies and strategies in place to mitigate the impact of disasters.<br>This is a quantitative correlational research where frequency counts, percentages,<br>mean, and Pearson r correlation was employed to assess whether there is a<br>significant relationship between the DRRM policies and the profiles of the 75<br>respondents from Barangay Buhangin, Butuan City. Samples were selected through<br>purposive sampling, which relies on data collected from respondents who were<br>eligible to participate in the study. This study revealed that the implementation of<br>DRRM policies in Barangay Buhangin was moderately implemented, which means<br>that the citizens were aware of the DRRM efforts of the barangay. While it can be<br>regarded as that, results showed that there were policies from different thematic<br>areas that need to be pushed through or are lacking in implementation. Therefore,<br>some policies need to be revisited to enhance and be given emphasis in order to be<br>highly effective. Hence, the researchers devised an action plan to suggest programs<br>and activities per DRRM thematic area in hope to intensify the implementation.</p>
