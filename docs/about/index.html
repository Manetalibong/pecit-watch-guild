About Us

Welcome to The Watch Guild Publication

The official student publication of Philippine Electronics and Communication Institute of Technology. We inform, inspire, and engage the PECIT community through student journalism.

Our story

More than a school paper

At The Watch Guild, we are a collective of student writers, editors, photographers, and creatives. Every article we publish reflects the curiosity, talent, and dedication of our team — from breaking campus news to research features and opinion columns.

We inform, inspire, and engage the PECIT community through accurate reporting, critical commentary, and creative storytelling.

  • Keep our community updated on events and campus life.
  • Share perspectives that spark dialogue and critical thinking.
  • Uphold press freedom and truthful reporting on campus.

Editorial promise

Truth. Insight. Student Voice.

We Deliver Truth

We uphold the highest standards of accuracy, fairness, and integrity in every article we publish. Misinformation has no place in our pages.

We Think Critically

We approach every story with depth and insight, asking tough questions, examining all sides, and thinking beyond the surface.

We Amplify Student Voices

We are a platform where every PECIT student can be seen and heard, regardless of background, course, or opinion.
