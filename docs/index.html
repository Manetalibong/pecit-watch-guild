Our Promise

Truth. Insight. Student Voice.

We Deliver Truth

We uphold the highest standards of accuracy, fairness, and integrity in every article we publish. Misinformation has no place in our pages.

We Think Critically

We approach every story with depth and insight, asking tough questions, examining all sides, and thinking beyond the surface.

We Amplify Student Voices

We are a platform where every PECIT student can be seen and heard, regardless of background, course, or opinion.

About Us

Welcome to The Watch Guild

At The Watch Guild, we are more than just a school publication. We are a collective of passionate student writers, editors, and creatives dedicated to informing, inspiring, and engaging our community.

We inform, inspire, and engage the PECIT community through accurate reporting, critical commentary, and creative storytelling.

  • Keep our community updated on the latest events and happenings.
  • Share perspectives that ignite imagination and dialogue.
  • Foster a sense of unity and pride within our school.
